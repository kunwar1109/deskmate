# BrewBox Help Center

BrewBox is a monthly coffee subscription. Every box contains two 250 g bags of freshly roasted specialty coffee from independent roasters.

## Plans and pricing

We offer three plans. The Classic plan costs ₹899 per month and includes two bags of medium roast coffee. The Explorer plan costs ₹1,299 per month and includes two single-origin coffees that change every month. The Office plan costs ₹3,499 per month and includes six bags, suitable for teams of up to 15 people.

All prices include GST. Shipping is free on every plan within India.

## Shipping and delivery

Boxes are roasted on the 1st of each month and shipped within 3 business days of roasting. Delivery usually takes 2 to 5 business days depending on your city. Metro cities such as Mumbai, Pune, Delhi and Bengaluru typically receive boxes within 2 to 3 business days.

You will receive a tracking link by email and SMS as soon as your box ships. We currently do not ship outside India.

## Pausing, skipping and cancelling

You can skip a month or pause your subscription for up to 3 months from Account > Subscription. Changes must be made before the 25th of the month to apply to the next box.

You can cancel at any time from Account > Subscription > Cancel plan. There are no cancellation fees. If you cancel after the 25th, the upcoming box has already been roasted and will still be shipped and charged.

## Refunds and damaged boxes

If your box arrives damaged or a bag is missing, email support@brewbox.example within 7 days of delivery with a photo of the package. We will send a free replacement or a full refund for that box, whichever you prefer.

We cannot refund boxes because of taste preferences, but our team can switch you to a different roast profile for next month at no cost.

## Grind options

Every plan is available as whole beans or ground coffee. Ground options are: espresso (fine), filter and pour-over (medium), and French press (coarse). You can change your grind setting any time before the 25th.

## Gifting

You can gift a 3-month, 6-month or 12-month subscription. Gift subscriptions are paid upfront, do not renew automatically, and include a printed gift card in the first box.
